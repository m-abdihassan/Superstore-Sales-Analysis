Superstore Sales Performance Report (2019-2022)
📌 Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive analysis of the Superstore sales data from 2019 to 2022. The goal is to identify sales trends, profitable regions, and key customer segments to help the business make data-driven decisions.
Author: Mohamed Abdi
Tool Used: Power BI, DAX, Data Modeling

📊 Key Insights (Quick Summary)
Between 2019 and 2022, the business experienced consistent growth:
Total Sales: $2.30M
Total Profit: $286.40K
Total Orders: 5,000
Profit Margin: 12.47%
Top Category: Technology (Highest revenue driver).
Best Region: West Region (Most profitable).
Top Segment: Consumer (Contributes the most profit).

📂 Data Source
The dataset used for this analysis was sourced from Kaggle:
Dataset:https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/timchant/supstore-dataset-2019-2022?resource

Description: This dataset contains sales, profit, and order information for a retail superstore.
🛠️ Data Process & Tools
To build this report, the following steps were taken:
Data Cleaning: Processing raw Superstore data to ensure accuracy.
Data Modeling: Connecting different tables for a functional relationship.
DAX Measures: Creating custom calculations for KPIs like Total Sales, Profit Margin, and Total Orders.
Visualizations: Designing an interactive dashboard in Power BI.

📈 Dashboard Features
The report is divided into several key sections:
Sales Trends: A line chart showing consistent upward growth over 4 years.
Category Analysis: Comparison between Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
Regional Performance: Deep dive into why the West is succeeding and why the Central region needs improvement.
Customer Segmentation: Analysis of Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office profitability.
Product Details: Tracking sales, profit, and quantity sold for specific products.

🚀 Conclusion & Recommendations
Invest in Success: Continue focusing on the Technology category and the West region.
Improve Weak Areas: Investigate why the Central region has lower profit margins (Check discounts and shipping costs).
Engagement: Create better deals for Corporate and Home Office segments to increase their profit contribution.
